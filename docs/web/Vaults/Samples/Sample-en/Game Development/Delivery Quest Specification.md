# Delivery Quest: A Letter for the Lighthouse

## Goal

Teach the player the basic delivery controls and introduce the relationship between the lighthouse keeper and the harbor town.

## Start conditions

- Inspect the notice board at the harbor.
- Accept the “A Letter for the Lighthouse” delivery request.

## Completion steps

1. Pick up the letter at the harbor.
2. Sail north to the lighthouse.
3. Give the letter to the keeper.
4. Return to the harbor and report the completed delivery.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] The destination appears on the map.
- [ ] The quest cannot be completed before the letter is collected.
- [ ] Talking to the keeper starts the dialogue and scene.
- [ ] The reward and completion state are saved.
