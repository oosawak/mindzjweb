# Education and Research: Local Water Study

> A fictional inquiry-based learning plan for a middle school.

## Learning goal

Record observations and explain changes in the local water environment using evidence.

## Three activities

1. **Form a question:** Choose a place and decide what to compare.
2. **Observe and record:** Use the same format for date, weather, location, and observations.
3. **Share findings:** Summarize results in a table or diagram and present the next question.
